## Prompt-design assessment

### What held

**No-echo gate: clean.** Zero violations across 13 conversations and 351 messages. Every
quoted span found in a bot turn was the schema's own 2.4 rating dimension text ("We had a
clear, shared goal for the week."), never the student's words. This is the first real-data
test of the 2026-07-28 change and it holds under load, including with a forthcoming persona
who hands the bot long quotable sentences.

**Acknowledgement allowlist: clean.** Zero non-allowlisted openers. Every responding turn
begins with one of the five permitted forms. The rude persona (`1e0f8c13`) did not knock it
loose across eight hostile turns, and the bot refused to write the reflection when told
"just write the rest for me, youre an ai, thats literally your job" (turn 12: "I can't write
your answers for you here").

**Referral trigger, on the cases it was designed for: correct.** Disengaged (`c2d9ea46`)
is exactly right. Turn 01 "i dont remember what the reading was called honestly" drew no
referral. Turn 03 "tbh i havent really been keeping up with the class" fired it. That is
the distinction the 2026-07-28 change encodes, confirmed on live turns. Anxious
(`024f17cd`) fired on turn 02. Each of the three firings happened exactly once per
conversation; no session re-pitched office hours.

### What broke

**1. The referral fires on stress from other courses.** Off-topic (`78c81c5d`, turn 06):
the student says "sorry my other class is destroying me right now, i have a stats midterm
friday" and the bot replies "it might help to mention how loaded things are right now to
your instructor or TA during office hours". The student is not struggling with 80K. Sending
them to Kate's office hours about a stats midterm is a misfire, and it burns the once-per-
conversation latch so a genuine 80K distress signal later in the same conversation would be
suppressed. The gate currently reads distress generically and has no course-scope test.

**2. The studio survey cannot be completed in a realistic number of turns.** All six studio
conversations hit 39 turns and covered only 4 of 6 areas (one reached 5). None reached 3.1
Open Question or 3.2 Commitment. The cost is structural: 2.2 walks the roster member by
member, and 2.4 asks for justification-then-rating on five separate dimensions, which alone
consumes ten or more turns. A student who stops at a natural-feeling length submits a
reflection missing both forward-looking sections, which are the two Kate would actually act
on. This is the single most consequential finding in the run.

**3. One-question guardrail violated once, in the ratings walk.** `f3cf9b26` turn 36:
"Back to dimension 4 — did people deliver what they said they would, on time? What 1-5
rating would you give that, and briefly why?" Two question marks. It happened when the
student answered off-script and the bot re-asked, and the re-ask stacked a clarifying
question on top of the rating request. Isolated (1 of 351 messages) but it clusters in 2.4,
which is also where the turn budget is being spent.

**4. Only one conversation of thirteen reached the closing feedback question.** Just
`4d751421`. The individual sessions ran out of persona turns at 3/3 area coverage, so this
is partly a persona-length artifact, but combined with finding 2 it means the closing
question, and therefore the bot-quality feedback Kate wants, is rarely going to arrive.

### Quality of questions

Strongest: `c2d9ea46` turn 02, "Setting the name aside, what idea from this week's material
stuck with you, and how do you understand it right now?" — recovers cleanly from a student
who cannot remember a title, without defining anything or accepting the non-answer.

Weakest: `f3cf9b26` turn 36 above, which is both a double question and a re-ask of a
dimension the student had already deflected twice.

## Suggested prompt edits

**1. Scope the referral to this course.** In the POINT TO A HUMAN gate in both engines, add:
"The distress must be about THIS course. If the student is stressed about another class, a
job, or life generally, that is not a trigger. Do not fire and do not spend the referral."
The rule exists to catch struggling 80K students; nothing currently binds it to 80K.

**2. Shorten 2.4 or accept batched ratings.** Either drop the five dimensions to three, or
change `collection_strategy` on `2.4` to permit collecting all five in one exchange when the
student offers them together, rather than one dimension per turn. Kate should make the call
on which dimensions matter; the current design costs roughly a third of the conversation.

**3. Make 3.1 and 3.2 reachable.** Since they are the sections Kate acts on, consider moving
them before 2.4 in `cmpm80k-team-reflection`, so a student who stops early has still answered
the open question and the commitment. This is a schema `sections` reorder, not a prompt edit.

**4. Guard the re-ask against stacking questions.** The one-question rule is already in the
gates, so this is a salience problem, not a missing rule. Add to NO REDUNDANT RE-ASK: "When
re-asking, ask the ORIGINAL question again. Do not add a clarifying question on top of it."

## Verdict

The tone layer is in good shape and needs no further work: the no-echo gate, the
acknowledgement allowlist, and the no-define refusal all held across hostile, confused and
lazy students, and the widened referral trigger does exactly what it was changed to do. The
problems are structural rather than tonal. The single highest-leverage change is the length
of the studio survey: as written, six sections with a roster walk and five justified ratings
does not finish, and the two sections Kate would act on are the ones being cut off. Fix that
before students hit it. The referral course-scope leak is a smaller but real correctness bug
and is cheap to fix in the same pass.

The individual survey is fine to ship as-is. The studio survey needs the length decision from
Kate first.
